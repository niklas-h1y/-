# Extension Setup: Zero-Width Vanisher 👻

Ready to turn any webpage on the internet into an invisible ghost town? This guide shows you exactly how to install and run the custom browser extension version of the tool.

The extension uses browser scripting privileges to safely step through a webpage's DOM tree, wiping plain text blocks into invisible Zero-Width Spaces (`U+200B`) while leaving HTML structures, layouts, and styles completely intact.

---

## 💻 Desktop Installation (Chrome, Brave, Edge, Opera)

1. **Prepare the Files:** Create a folder on your computer named `Zero_Width_Vanisher`. Save the three extension files (`manifest.json`, `popup.html`, and `popup.js`) inside it.
2. **Open Extensions Portal:** Navigate to `chrome://extensions/` in your browser URL bar.
3. **Turn on Developer Mode:** Toggle the **Developer mode** switch in the top-right corner to **ON**.
4. **Deploy the Directory:** Click the **Load unpacked** button in the top-left corner.
5. **Select Folder:** Choose your `Zero_Width_Vanisher` folder. 

🚀 *The ghost icon will pop up in your browser menu. Open any site, click the button, and watch the text vanish into thin air!*

---

## 📱 Mobile Installation (Android / Tablet)

Standard mobile browsers do not allow custom unpacked installations, but you can run this text-wiper on mobile devices using an extension-supported client:

1. **Get your Environment:** Download **Lemur Browser** or **Mises Browser** directly from the official Google Play Store.
2. **Download your Files:** Compress your repository folder into a standard `.zip` archive on your mobile device.
3. **Open Extensions Manager:** In Lemur Browser, tap the **Shield/Extensions** icon at the top right next to the menu dots.
4. **Enable Developer Settings:** Switch the **Entwicklermodus (Developer Mode)** toggle button to **ON**.
5. **Import ZIP:** Click the **"+ (from zip)"** or **"Local"** upload button and select your downloaded repository ZIP file.

🚀 *The tool will load instantly. Open any page, click your top bar extension icon, select **Zero-Width Vanisher**, and hit "Vanish This Page"!*
